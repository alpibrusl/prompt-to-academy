Prompt to Judgement

Teaching a generation to specify, to check, and to answer for work it did not build

A book, and the cohort curriculum derived from it. It teaches children to use AI productively and safely by first making them do the thing themselves — small and by hand — and only then letting them delegate it and check the result.
