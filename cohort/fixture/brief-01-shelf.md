# Brief: the shelf

We have one shelf of books in the room. People take them and mostly bring
them back. Right now nobody can tell what is on it without walking over.

Make something that fixes that.

---

*That is the whole brief, and it is typical. Every hard decision in it has
been left to you: what "something" is, what "on the shelf" means when a book
is out on loan, who is meant to use it, what happens when two people update
it at once, and whether "mostly bring them back" is a problem you are being
asked to solve or a fact you are being asked to live with.*

*Write the specification. Hand it to someone who will build it without
talking to you.*
