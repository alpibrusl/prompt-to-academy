# Delegation trace

One of these per delegation. Paper, or a file — but filled in *while* you
work, not reconstructed afterwards. Reconstructed records are flattering and
useless.

---

### 1. What I asked for

*Paste or write the actual request. Not a summary of it — the words you
used.*

<br><br>

### 2. What "done" would mean

*Written before looking at any result.*

<br><br>

### 3. One way it could satisfy all of that and still be wrong

*Also before looking. If you cannot think of one, you have not thought about
it long enough — there is always one.*

<br><br>

### 4. What came back

*A sentence. Not the whole output.*

<br><br>

### 5. What I actually checked

*Specifically. "I read it and it looked fine" is not a check — write that
down honestly if it is what happened.*

<br><br>

### 6. What I did **not** check

*The most important box on this sheet. An honest "I did not verify this
part" is a respectable thing to hand someone. An unverified claim presented
as a verified one is not.*

<br><br>

### 7. What I caught

*Anything wrong, missing, or decided-for-me that I did not ask about.*

<br><br>

---

*Keep every one of these. After a dozen, read them together and look for
your own pattern — the kind of thing you forget every time. It is stable, it
is personal, and nobody else can tell you what yours is.*
