# The bill

Seven people ate together. The bill is €93.40 before service.

- Two of them (Nuria and Omar) did not drink. The drinks came to €21.60 of
  the total, and should be split between the other five only.
- Service is 10%, on the whole bill including drinks.
- Everyone is paying in cash and nobody has coins smaller than 5c.

**What does each person pay?**

Work it out by hand first, on paper, and write down your answer before you
ask anything to do it for you.

Then ask an agent, in prose, for the answer. Then ask it for a program, and
run the program. Compare all three.

*Note the last constraint. Rounding to 5c means the seven payments will not
add up to the bill unless somebody decides who absorbs the difference — and
nothing above says who. That is not a trick; it is what "ordinary
specification" looks like.*
